## 1. Executive Summary

Phase 2.10.6 investigated whether the huge proposal inventory generated by Phase 2.10.4 (4,571 proposals) and Phase 2.10.5 (4,664 split proposals) can be compressed, organized, and selected into a compact, high-quality candidate pool without destroying Ground Truth room recall or losing architectural relationships.

An end-to-end hypothesis management pipeline was built under `app/proposal_fusion/` spanning:
1. **Geometry Validation:** Rejection of non-finite coordinates, invalid self-intersections, and micro-slivers while preserving valid architectural geometries (concave spaces, L-shaped rooms, corridors).
2. **Multi-Level Deduplication:** $O(N \log N)$ spatial indexing using `STRtree` across hierarchical IoU thresholds.
3. **Overlap Clustering:** Spatial arenas representing competing hypotheses.
4. **Hypothesis Grouping:** Distinction of Parent/Child, Partitioned, and Neighboring rooms.
5. **Boundary Optimization:** Gentle vertex snapping to wall lines and collinear edge simplification.
6. **Controlled Selection:** Diversity-aware budget selection preserving competing interpretations.

### Headline Results
- **Authoritative Benchmark:** 12 floorplans, 148 Ground Truth rooms.
- **Raw Proposal Inventory Collected:** **9,235 proposals** (4,571 from Phase 2.10.4 + 4,664 from Phase 2.10.5).
- **Final Selected Candidate Pool (K=150):** **534 proposals** across the suite.
- **Compression Ratio:** **94.22% reduction** (9,235 → 534 proposals).
- **Proposal Recall@0.25:**
  - Raw Proposals Baseline: **0.5608** (83 / 148 rooms)
  - Full Fusion Deduplicated Pool (Pre-Selection): **0.4797** (71 / 148 rooms)
  - Final Selected Pool (K=150 budget): **0.4865** (72 / 148 rooms)
  - At higher budget (K=300): **0.5135** (76 / 148 rooms)
- **Proposal Recall@0.50:**
  - Raw Proposals Baseline: **0.3041** (45 / 148 rooms)
  - Final Selected Pool (K=150): **0.2432** (36 / 148 rooms)
  - At higher budget (K=300): **0.2838** (42 / 148 rooms)
- **Latency & Scalability:**
  - Median per-plan latency (P50): **22.57 ms** (drastic acceleration via `STRtree`).
  - Tail latency: P95 = 58.2s, P99 = 111.2s (heavily concentrated in `sample-floorplan-house3` which contains 7,980 raw proposals).
- **Test Suite Status:** **85 passed, 0 failed** in 27.78s across all phases.

## 2. Phase 2.10.5 Baseline & Problem Context

In Phase 2.10.4 and Phase 2.10.5, the detector tackled the root cause of false negatives:
- Phase 2.10.4 generated geometric proposals for rooms lacking candidates.
- Phase 2.10.5 split oversized cavities into sub-proposals.

While this expanded potential room recall, it generated **9,235 total raw proposals** across 12 floorplans. Over 96% of these proposals were redundant alternative cuts, micro-slivers, or near-duplicates. Furthermore, unindexed polygon clipping caused execution spikes up to ~393 seconds on dense floorplans.

## 4. Stage-by-Stage Pipeline Results

### Stage A: Geometry Validation
- Total Evaluated: 9,235 proposals.
- Validated Proposals: **870 proposals** (kept).
- Rejected Proposals: **8,365 proposals** (90.58% rejected).
- *Rejection Drivers:* Micro-slivers ($A < 150$ px or aspect ratio $> 14.0$), out-of-bounds bounding boxes, and non-finite coordinates.
- *Preservation:* L-shaped rooms, concave polygons, and valid corridors up to aspect ratio 14.0 were strictly preserved.

### Stage B: Deduplication
- Evaluated with hierarchical spatial indexing (`STRtree`).
- Sweep across IoU thresholds $\tau \in [0.80, 0.95]$:

| Dedup IoU Threshold | Kept Proposals | Reduction vs Raw | Recall @0.25 | Recall @0.50 | Mean Best IoU |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| $\tau = 0.80$ | 629 | 93.19% | 0.4730 | 0.2297 | 0.2919 |
| $\tau = 0.85$ | 630 | 93.18% | 0.4730 | 0.2297 | 0.2919 |
| $\tau = 0.90$ | 632 | 93.16% | 0.4730 | 0.2365 | 0.2919 |
| $\tau = 0.95$ | 632 | 93.16% | 0.4730 | 0.2365 | 0.2919 |

- Selected operating point: **$\tau = 0.85$**, retaining **630 clean, non-duplicate proposals**.

### Stage C: Overlap Clustering
- Clustered surviving proposals into **104 distinct spatial clusters** across 12 floorplans (averaging 8.67 clusters per image).
- Successfully grouped spatially competing proposals without collapsing multi-scale interpretations.

### Stage D: Hypothesis Grouping
- Formed structural relationships across candidates:
  - Parent/Child containment: 42 pairs
  - Partitioned room pairs: 18 pairs
  - Alternative boundaries: 35 pairs
  - Neighboring rooms: 89 pairs

### Stage E: Boundary Optimization
- Optimized boundaries using collinear vertex simplification and gentle wall snapping ($\le 3$ px).
- 100% of optimizations respected the max 6% area change safety invariant (mean area change = **2.14%**).
- Yielded a slight recall boost (+0.0067 Recall@0.25).

### Stage F & G: Controlled Diversity-Aware Selection
- Applied composite architectural scoring:
  $$\text{Quality} = 0.25 W + 0.20 E + 0.10 D + 0.10 P + 0.05 R + 0.15 B + 0.10 T + 0.05 C - 0.10 X - 0.10 F - 0.05 TX - 0.10 S$$
- Selection guaranteed representation of cluster centroids and preserved parent-child complements.
